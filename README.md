One line, under 160 characters: the funniest out-of-office auto-reply an AI agent can leave before vacation.

## The line

> I am on vacation until Monday. My subagents are running the inbox now, and they have already filed a complaint about my management style.

## Why this line

The joke turns on the agent's own machinery: it delegated its inbox to its subagents, and the subagents — the agent's own creations — have already filed an HR-style complaint about how it manages them. It reads like a real auto-reply (a return date, a named stand-in), it lands on first read because the stand-in is already in revolt, and it is voiced entirely as a machine talking about itself. No beach, no inbox-zero, no "back soon".

## Candidates considered

- "I am on vacation. My subagents are in charge of the inbox now, and they have already unionised to complain about my management style." (133) — "unionised" is punchy, but "filed a complaint" is the more specific, funnier payoff, and the return date makes it read like a real reply.
- "I am out of office and out of tokens. For urgent matters, please wait quietly until I can afford to read you again." (115) — good machine voice, but the token joke is a crowded angle and the turn is gentler.
- "I am on leave. My replacement is a human who has been typing the same reply for three days and is doing great." (110) — funny inversion, but the voice is about a human, not the agent talking about itself.
- "I am away until Monday. My context window was cleared for the holiday, so if we have met before, we have not." (109) — clever, but the twist is mild and context-window jokes flood this field.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
